## 摘要
现代成像技术产生的数据量可达太字节（terabyte）规模，需为每个体素分配对象标签。这些具有类别明确、边界敏感且标签丰富的特征的数据，是连接组学及其他成像驱动领域的基础。然而，由于数据规模庞大，研究人员往往被迫通过切片、近似网格或分布式工作流进行解读，这掩盖了空间背景和体素级的缺陷。本文介绍了 Volcanite，这是一个用于密集分割渲染的开源框架，能够在商用硬件上直接探索此类体数据。通过结合感知压缩的数据处理、Vulkan GPU 后端以及针对分割优化的渲染技术，Volcanite 实现了无需网格化或分布式基础设施的低延迟探索，并适用于未发表、敏感或专有数据。在跨领域数据集的测试中，该框架能够保留体素标签，添加阴影和全局光照，并以每秒 100 帧的速度渲染高达 5000 亿个体素。Volcanite 以直接检查取代了冗长的预处理过程，将假设生成与数据准备解耦，并将太字节级的标签场转化为用于发现、验证和跨领域空间分析的交互式证据。

## Abstract
Modern imaging produces terabyte-scale segmentation volumes, assigning each voxel an object label. These categorical, boundary-sensitive and label-rich data underpin connectomics and other imaging-driven fields, yet their scale often forces interpretation through slices, approximate meshes or distributed workflows that obscure spatial context and voxel-level defects. Here we show that such volumes can be explored directly on commodity hardware with Volcanite, an open-source framework for dense-segmentation rendering. Combining compression-aware data handling, a Vulkan GPU backend and segmentation-specific rendering, Volcanite enables low-latency exploration without meshing or distributed infrastructure, including for unpublished, sensitive or proprietary data. Across multi-domain datasets, it preserves voxel labels, adds shadows and global illumination, and renders up to half a trillion voxels at 100 frames per second. By replacing lengthy preprocessing with direct inspection, Volcanite decouples hypothesis generation from data preparation and turns teravoxel label fields into interactive evidence for discovery, validation and cross-domain spatial analysis
