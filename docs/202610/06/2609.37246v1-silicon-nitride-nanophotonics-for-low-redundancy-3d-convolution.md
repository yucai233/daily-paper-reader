## 摘要
三维（3D）卷积能够提取高维数据中的相关性，但重叠的感受野会导致大量冗余的数据移动。本文展示了一种氮化硅3D光卷积加速器（3D-OCA），它通过坐标感知的波长-群延迟映射来重构感受野。确定性串行化的输入张量被广播到多个波长通道上，并通过啁啾波导布拉格光栅（CWBG）补偿与3D卷积核坐标相关的时间偏移。这种设计无需重复重排和加载共享的输入样本，即可连续形成相邻的感受野。集成的CWBG可提供 2691 ps 的差分群延迟和 124.8 ps/nm 的色散。在 20 Gbaud 速率下，对 Indian Pines 数据进行的空间-光谱处理实现了高达 0.997 决定系数的卷积一致性，分类准确率达到 97.9%（相比之下数字计算为 98.9%）。在 10 Gbaud 速率下，该光卷积层保留了时空特征，并在四分类 KTH 视频识别任务中实现了 92.5% 的准确率。这些结果表明，利用相同的光延迟架构可以在光谱和时间数据维度上实现低冗余的流式3D卷积。

## Abstract
Three-dimensional (3D) convolution extracts correlations in high-dimensional data, but overlapping receptive fields introduce substantial redundant data movement. Here, we demonstrate a silicon nitride 3D optical convolution accelerator (3D-OCA) that reconstructs receptive fields through coordinate-aware wavelength-to-group-delay mapping. A deterministically serialized input tensor is broadcast onto multiple wavelength channels, and a chirped waveguide Bragg grating (CWBG) compensates the temporal offsets associated with 3D kernel coordinates. This arrangement continuously forms neighboring receptive fields without repeatedly rearranging and loading their shared input samples. The integrated CWBG provides a differential group delay of 2691 ps and a dispersion of 124.8 ps/nm. At 20 Gbaud, spatial-spectral processing of Indian Pines data yields convolution agreement with a coefficient of determination up to 0.997 and 97.9% classification accuracy, compared with 98.9% digitally. At 10 Gbaud, the optical convolution layer preserves spatiotemporal features and achieves 92.5% accuracy on a four-class KTH video-recognition task. These results establish low-redundancy streaming 3D convolution across spectral and temporal data dimensions using the same optical delay architecture.
