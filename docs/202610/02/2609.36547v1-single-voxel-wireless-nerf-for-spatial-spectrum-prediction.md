## 摘要
跨多个空间方向的无线信道测量对于射频（RF）数字孪生和通信感知一体化等人工智能驱动的应用至关重要。然而，在大型场景中采集信道数据非常耗时耗力。无线神经辐射场（Wireless NeRFs）通过从稀疏测量中学习传播行为，并合成未见位置的信道空间频谱幅度，从而解决了这一挑战。然而，现有的无线 NeRF 继承了视觉 NeRF 的密集体素采样方式，这需要大量的计算资源。本文探讨了这种密集采样对于预测无线空间频谱幅度是否必要。我们通过实证研究表明，无线 NeRF 在该任务中存在过参数化问题，并引入了 SV-INGP，这是一种基于即时神经图形基元（INGP）的稀疏体素采样变体。在真实世界和模拟数据集上的实验结果显示，SV-INGP 在保持与 NeRF2 基准模型相当的结构相似性指数（SSIM）中位数的同时，将训练时间缩短了 184 倍。这些结果在视距（LoS）和非视距（NLoS）场景、Sub-6GHz 和毫米波频段以及天线阵列锥削配置中均具有普适性，为射频数字孪生提供了一条更简单、更高效的设计路径。

## Abstract
Wireless channel measurements across multiple spatial directions are crucial for AI-driven applications, such as RF digital twins and integrated communication and sensing. However, collecting channel data across large scenes is labor-intensive. Wireless NeRFs address this challenge by learning propagation behavior from sparse measurements and synthesizing channel spatial spectrum magnitude at unseen locations. However, existing wireless NeRFs inherit dense volumetric sampling from vision NeRFs, which requires substantial computation. This paper asks whether such dense sampling is necessary for predicting magnitudes of the wireless spatial spectrum. We empirically show that wireless NeRFs are over-parameterized for this task and introduce SV-INGP, a sparse volumetric sampling variant of Instant Neural Graphics Primitives (INGP). Across real-world and simulated datasets, SV-INGP matches the median Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM) of the NeRF2 baseline while reducing training time by 184x. These results generalize across LoS and NLoS scenes, sub-6 and millimeter-wave frequencies, and antenna array tapering configurations, suggesting a simpler and more efficient design path for RF digital twins
