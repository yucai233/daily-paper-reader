## 摘要
我们基于微面理论推导了一种新型的类漫反射 BRDF。其主要创新点在于，它为评估和重要性采样提供了一个初等表达式，同时在 Smith 遮蔽假设下精确考虑了所有散射阶数。因此，这是首个解决此类构造是否可行问题的研究。在实践中，该 BRDF 在法向入射时产生朗伯反射，并随着入射角趋于掠射角，逐渐将反射光集中在镜面反射方向。我们通过将渲染结果与朗伯模型及最先进的随机微面 BRDF 进行对比，展示了这一效果。

## Abstract
We derive a novel diffuse-like BRDF based on microfacet theory. Its main novelty lies in the fact that it admits an elementary expression for both evaluation and importance sampling, while accounting exactly for all scattering orders under the Smith shadowing assumption. As such, it is the first to settle the question of whether such a construction was possible. In practice, the BRDF produces Lambertian reflectance at normal incidence and progressively concentrates reflected light around the mirror direction as incidence approaches grazing. We showcase this effect by comparing rendering results against Lambertian and state-of-the-art stochastic microfacet BRDFs.
